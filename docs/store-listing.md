# App Store listing — 1.2.0

Copy from here into App Store Connect. Kept in the repo so the listing is
versioned alongside the features it describes; update it when the app changes.

The pitch, everywhere it fits: Plato tracks workouts and cardio, and nothing
pops up, ever. Every claim below is checked against the app: no ads, no
rating, upgrade or notification prompts (Plato sends no notifications at all),
no purchases, no analytics or tracking code, and no account needed. Keep it
that way, or change the copy. Apple pulls apps whose listing promises what
they don't do (guideline 2.3.1).

"Pop-ups" means the ones people hate. iOS still asks for Health and Motion
access the first time you start cardio, and a delete still asks "Delete
workout?". Those are Apple's sheets or your own taps, so the copy promises
no pop-ups *asking you to* rate, upgrade, subscribe or turn on notifications.

Nothing will ever pop up asking you to rate the app, upgrade, start a trial or turn on notifications. Not on your first workout, not on your five hundredth.

LOG LIFTS FAST

• An optional rest timer between sets, from 30 seconds to 5 minutes

TRAIN SMARTER
• Every lift shows your best set, your estimated one-rep max and a chart of where it's heading

• Full workout history, a tap away

CARDIO

APPLE WATCH
• Log the workout in progress from your wrist: the next set's weight and reps are ready, the Digital Crown adjusts them, and one tap logs it
• The rest countdown, right on your wrist
• Start a template from the watch
• Put Plato on your watch face

LIVE ACTIVITY

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

PLAN YOUR TRAINING
• Reusable workout templates, started in one tap

BODY WEIGHT

MAKE IT YOURS

YOUR DATA

Plato is for training. Everything else got cut.

## Notes for App Review

```
The whole app is usable as a guest with no account, so no demo credentials
are needed — tap "Continue as guest" on the sign-in screen.

Sign in with Apple, Google and email/password are all supported for users who
want cloud sync. Account deletion is in Profile → Delete account.

Body weight is optional, entered by hand, and used only to chart the user's
own weight and to value bodyweight exercises.

Apple Health is used only for cardio sessions started in Plato: on the
Workouts tab, Start cardio → Run → Start. With an Apple Watch paired, the
watch tracks the session (heart rate, energy, distance) and saves it to Health
as a workout. Plato never reads workouts recorded by other apps. Without a
watch, Health isn't used: a run, walk or hike gets its distance from the
iPhone's step counter (Motion & Fitness), and other sessions run on the
phone's clock.

The Apple Watch app logs the workout in progress on the iPhone. Start one on
the phone (Workouts → Start workout → Start blank workout, then add an
exercise) and it appears on the watch, ready to log sets. With no workout
going, the watch lists your templates to start one, and Start → Cardio starts
a session from the wrist.

The privacy policy and support contact are in the app under Profile → Help.
```
